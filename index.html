Questions before you connect

What does PointBase actually do?

It keeps every fact about your business — hours, menu, phone, reviews, photos — verified and consistent everywhere AI and customers look, and answers on your behalf when an agent asks.

Do I need another dashboard to use it?

No. You can run it from our console if you want, but the point is that it works in the background and through the AI assistants you already use.

Can I use it through ChatGPT or another assistant?

Yes. Any connected assistant can query your presence and trigger fixes through our agent layer.

Do I need to manage it every day?

No. Connect once and it maintains itself — syncing changes, replying to reviews, and flagging anything that needs you.

What kind of business is it for?

Any business people find by place — restaurants, hotels, clinics, banks, gyms, retail, EV charging — from a single site to thousands.

Who is it not for?

Purely online businesses with no physical location or local footprint won't get much from it.

Does it replace my existing systems?

No — it sits on top of them, reconciling what they already say into one verified source of truth.

Does it work for one location and for hundreds?

The same truth model scales from one to thousands, with roles, approvals, and per-location reporting built in.
