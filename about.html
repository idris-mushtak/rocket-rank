Our mission

Make the machines that answer for you tell the truth

For two decades, being found meant ranking on a page of links. That era is ending. Customers ask a question and get a single answer — from a model that decided, on their behalf, which business to trust.

We believe every business deserves to be represented accurately in that answer, no matter its size. PointBase gives owners and multi-location brands one place to verify their facts and one signal that flows to every system that matters — so the truth about your business travels with it, wherever AI takes it.
